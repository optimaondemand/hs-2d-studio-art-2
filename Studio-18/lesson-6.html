STUDIO 18 · LESSON 6 · FINAL LESSON OF THE COURSE
Refine, Reflect & Present
Polish the exhibition, run one last self-critique, and close out the whole year. ~55 min + studio time
🔍 PART 1 · CRITIQUE THE EXHIBITION, NOT JUST THE ART

You have run this same critique on other artists' work all year, and on your own single pieces. This time, run it on the exhibition as a whole, the actual object you would hand a stranger.

D · Describe

Open your exhibition from the start, as a stranger would. What order do the works actually appear in? What text appears before any artwork?

A · Analyze

Check your three curatorial jobs from Lesson 2. Does the selection feel deliberate? Does the sequence build toward something? Does every label have all four parts?

I · Interpret

Does the whole thing, images plus labels plus statement, add up to a clear sense of who you are as an artist? Or does it feel like a folder of separate files?

J · Judge

What one change would most improve it? Usually it is tightening the short list further, fixing an inconsistent label, or sharpening one vague sentence in the statement.

💭 REVISION CHECK-IN · NO WRONG ANSWER
You look at your assembled exhibition and it feels more like a pile of files than a show. What is the most likely fix?
✨ PART 2 · LAST PASS

Before you submit, do one full pass through your exhibition, start to finish, checking for these specific things.

💡 THE LAST-PASS CHECKLIST
Every image is straight-on, well-lit, and cropped tight (Lesson 4). Every label has all four parts and matches the piece it sits beside. The sequence still makes sense on a fresh read. Your artist statement is proofread and covers all four parts from Lesson 4. Your name and the exhibition's title appear on the title slide or section.

🏆 THIS IS THE LAST GRADED WORK OF THE COURSE
Everything you learned in Studios 1 through 17 is only visible to anyone else through how well you present it here. Give this last piece the same care you gave your very first observational drawing.
